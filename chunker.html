Text chunker for LLMs

Split long documents into overlapping, token-sized chunks for search and retrieval.

Token counts are estimates (about 4 characters per token). Splits happen at paragraph and sentence boundaries.

What it is for

Models can only read so much at once, and document-search systems (often called RAG) work by splitting documents into chunks, finding the few chunks relevant to a question, and sending only those. This tool does the splitting step, with a token budget per chunk and an optional overlap.

Example

A 100,000-token manual and 20 questions: sending the whole manual each time costs 2,000,000 input tokens, while sending the three most relevant 500-token chunks per question costs 30,000. The chunking itself costs nothing; the saving comes from sending less.

Why overlap?

An idea often straddles a boundary. With a 50-token overlap, the end of each chunk is repeated at the start of the next, so a sentence cut at the boundary still appears whole in at least one chunk.

Questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. Splitting happens in your browser.

How big should chunks be?

A common starting point is 300 to 800 tokens with 10 to 15 percent overlap. Smaller chunks give more precise matches, larger chunks keep more context.

Are the token counts exact?

No, they are estimates of about 4 characters per token. Use the Token counter for exact OpenAI counts.
