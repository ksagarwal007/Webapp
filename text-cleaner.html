AI Text Cleaner

Strip Markdown, invisible characters and em dashes from AI-generated text.

What It Is For

Text copied from AI tools often carries Markdown symbols, invisible characters and heavy use of em dashes that look odd when pasted into email, documents or forms. This tool cleans it up in one step and tells you what it changed.

Questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. Cleaning happens in your browser.

Will it break names like my_variable_name?

No. Underscores are only treated as italics when they wrap a whole word or phrase, so snake_case names are left alone.

Does it change ranges like 2010–2020?

No. En dashes are only replaced when they have spaces on both sides.

What are invisible characters?

Zero-width spaces, soft hyphens and similar marks that some tools insert. They are invisible but can break searches, links and word counts.
