What it is for

Chatbots often cap file size or mangle tables, and copy-pasting from a PDF drags in headers, footers, page numbers and broken lines. This tool extracts just the text, so you can paste only what you need and spend fewer tokens.

Questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. The PDF is read in your browser and never leaves your device.

Why does a PDF come out empty?

It is probably a scan, which is an image with no text layer. It needs OCR, which this tool does not include.

What does the header and footer option do?

It drops short lines that repeat on at least 40 percent of the pages, such as running titles and page numbers. Turn it off if it removes something you need.

Can I paste a web page?

Yes. Save the page as HTML or use View Source, then paste it. Menus, scripts and footers are stripped and headings are kept.
