Prompt Template Builder

Write reusable prompts with variables, save them, and share them as a link. Everything stays in your browser.

What It Is For

When you send the same kind of prompt again and again, a template keeps the wording consistent and stops details being forgotten. Write the template once with {{variables}}, fill in the boxes each time and copy the finished prompt.

Example

A support team saves one template for replying to customers, with boxes for tone, policy and the customer message, then fills in three boxes for each new ticket.

Questions

Where are my saved templates stored?

In your browser on this device only. Use Export all to back them up or move them to another device.

How do share links work?

The template is encoded inside the link itself, so nothing is stored on a server. Anyone with the link can read it, so keep secrets out of templates. Long templates make long links.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
