How the forecast works

Chatbots and agents re-send the system prompt and the whole history on every turn, so cost grows faster than the number of turns. The forecaster adds that up per turn, caps it at the model's context window, and prices input and output separately. Prompt batches are a single call repeated at your volume.

Questions

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser. The share link stores only your settings, never pasted text.

How accurate is it?

It is a model, not a quote. The badge shows whether token counts are exact (OpenAI, when the tokenizer loads) or estimated. Real bills also depend on retries, tool calls and provider rules.

What does prompt caching change here?

With caching on, the repeated part of each turn is billed at a reduced rate. The headline assumes a warm cache, and the Cache warmth chart shows how lower traffic erodes the saving.

Where do prices come from?

From the open-source LiteLLM catalog, refreshed daily, and not verified against provider billing. The footer line at the bottom of the forecast shows the date.
