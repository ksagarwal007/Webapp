About {{SITE}}

Free, private tools and plain-English guides for people building with AI.

What this site is

{{SITE}} is a small set of tools for the everyday questions that come up when you work with large language models: how many tokens is this text, what will this feature cost each month, which model is cheapest for the job, and how do I shorten a prompt without losing meaning.

How it works

The tools run in your browser. The text, PDFs, chats and JSON you use with them are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. There is no account to create. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Where the numbers come from

  • API prices come from an open-source model price catalog and are refreshed automatically every day. They are not checked against provider billing, so treat them as a guide and confirm on the provider's own pricing page before you commit. The Model Prices page shows the date of the last refresh.
  • Subscription prices for consumer plans, such as those in our blog guides, are checked by hand and carry their own “checked” date, because they change rarely and have no feed to read from.
  • Token counts are exact for OpenAI models. For other model families they are estimates, and the tools say so.

About the blog

The blog publishes guides on AI costs, tokens and prompts. Each article shows when it was published and, where it quotes prices, when those prices were last checked. Articles are written under the site name, not an individual byline.

Corrections and feedback

If a price, a number or an explanation looks wrong, email {{EMAIL}} with the page and what you saw. We correct errors and update the date on the article when we do.
