The short version

{{SITE}} runs in your browser. The text, PDFs, chats and JSON you use with the tools are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers or stored by us.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not receive the content you paste, upload or export.
  • Share links keep your settings inside the link itself (after the # sign), which browsers do not send to servers.

What we do receive

Like any website, our hosting provider records standard server logs (for example your IP address, the pages requested and the time) to keep the service running and secure. Your browser also downloads some libraries and fonts from public content delivery networks, which see your IP address when you load a page that needs them.

Your theme choice

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Price data

The tools fetch a small file of model prices from this site. No personal information is involved.
